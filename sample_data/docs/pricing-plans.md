# Plans and Pricing

All prices are per workspace per month, billed in USD or INR.

## Starter

$19 per month (INR 1,499). One project, 100 compute hours, 10 GB storage, community support.

## Growth

$99 per month (INR 7,999). Ten projects, 1,000 compute hours, 100 GB storage, email support with
a 1 business day response target, and single sign-on with Google Workspace.

## Scale

$499 per month (INR 39,999). Unlimited projects, 10,000 compute hours, 1 TB storage, 24x7 chat
support, SAML single sign-on, audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Extra usage

Extra compute hours cost $0.04 per hour. Extra storage costs $0.10 per GB per month. Paying
annually gives two months free on every plan.
