# Support and Uptime SLA

## Support response targets

| Plan | Channel | First response target |
|------|---------|-----------------------|
| Starter | Community forum | Best effort |
| Growth | Email | 1 business day |
| Scale | 24x7 chat and email | 1 hour for Severity 1, 4 hours for Severity 2 |
| Enterprise | Dedicated engineer | 15 minutes for Severity 1 |

Severity 1 means production is down or data is at risk. Severity 2 means a major feature is
degraded with no workaround.

## Uptime commitment

The Scale plan includes a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. If monthly uptime falls below 99.95%,
Scale customers receive a 10% service credit; below 99.0%, a 25% service credit. Credits must be
claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Starter and Growth plans have no
uptime SLA.
