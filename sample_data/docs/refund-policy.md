# Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 March 2026. This policy replaces the 2024 refund policy.

## Eligibility

Customers on the Starter, Growth and Scale plans can request a full refund within 30 days of
their first payment. Annual plans are refunded pro rata after the first 30 days, minus a
10% processing fee. Monthly plans are not refunded after 30 days; cancellation stops the next
renewal instead.

Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms in the signed order form, not this policy.

## How to request a refund

Open Billing > Invoices, select the invoice and click "Request refund". Refunds are reviewed
within 2 business days and paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business
days. Refunds in INR are processed through Razorpay; all other currencies go through Stripe.

## Exceptions

Usage-based overages (extra compute hours and extra storage) are never refundable. Accounts
suspended for abuse under the Acceptable Use Policy lose refund eligibility.
